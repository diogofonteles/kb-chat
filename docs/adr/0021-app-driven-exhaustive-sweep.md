# 0021 — The exhaustive profile: an app-driven sweep with coverage measured by the app

- Status: accepted (2026-10-27)
- Amends: [0013](0013-exhaustive-research-grep-tools-and-compaction.md) (the exhaustive profile no longer lets the model decide when the sweep is complete; the coverage line is the app's), [0014](0014-research-subagents.md) (superseded for the exhaustive profile: no `delegate`, no "pesquisador" sub-agent)
- Drivers: KBC-030; the owner's decision of 2026-10-27 ("in Exaustivo the app must guarantee the sweep, for every provider"), Q4 grounding, Q3 cost

## Context

In production (2026-10-27) the owner asked the exhaustive profile, on a free OpenRouter model, for every book José Laércio do Egito cites as a reference across his work. The turn ran 88 steps for ~550 s, paged the authorship listing (2,555 documents), read 38 of them and answered with "**Cobertura:** varri 2.555 documentos…". The claim was false. On the Claude subscription the same question delegated to sub-researchers and swept properly. Two causes: under ADR 0013/0014 the **model** decides when the coverage is complete (weak and free models stop early), and the coverage line is **written by the model**, so it can be wrong.

Measured on the real vault (read-only, no model, 2026-10-27; `work/KBC-030/evidence/real-vault.md`): the topic has 2,986 cataloged documents; `authorship = mestre-laercio` resolves to **2,555** documents with **28.3 million** characters of body. Read whole, that is 620 batches for a 32k-token model and 120 batches for a subscription model. Scanning first for seven broad expressions (`livro`, `obra`, `leiam`, `leia`, `recomendo`, `bibliografia`, `autor`) leaves 1,363 documents with occurrences and 7.7 million characters around them: 160 batches (32k) or 31 (subscription). Resolving the set and the batch plan takes 0.6–5 s.

## Decision

**Plan → sweep → answer, run by the app, the same for every runner.**

1. **Plan.** In the exhaustive profile the orchestrator has the research tools plus `plan_sweep { target: { authorship?, entity?, source?, subjects? }, phrases?, extract }` (the contract's `sweepPlanSchema`) and at most 12 tool steps (`KB_CHAT_EXHAUSTIVE_STEPS` can only lower it). Text it writes before planning is retracted. Without a plan (it answered, or the steps ran out) the **default plan** applies: `authorship: mestre-laercio` when the folded question names "laercio" or "mestre", otherwise the whole topic; no phrases (every document read whole); the question as the extraction. A continuation ("Continuar varredura") skips planning and uses the cursor's plan.
2. **Resolve and batch** (`SweepPlanner`, read-only). The set is the catalog entries the target accepts, in id order (the cursor's order). With phrases, find_text's folded corpus scans every body (those documents count as examined inside) and only windows of ±1,500 characters around occurrences, merged, go to the batches; documents without occurrences are scanned, not read. Without phrases every body is read whole, in windows no larger than a batch. A batch holds up to `batchChars` = half the model's context in characters (1.6 characters per context token, 24,000–300,000): 32k tokens (free routers, one's own OpenRouter model, unknown) → 51,200; 128k (OpenAI) → 204,800; 200k (Anthropic) and 1M (Gemini) → 300,000; the subscription CLIs → 160k → 256,000.
3. **Map.** Each batch is **one model call without tools**: the batch reader prompt, the question, the extraction and the fenced documents labelled `[Dk]`. It answers one finding per line (`- [Dk] finding — “quote”`, or `NENHUM`). Labels outside the batch are dropped; findings are numbered in the turn's `SourceBook` (the [n] shared with the sources and the answer) and deduplicated per document and folded text. Batches run 3 at a time on per-token models and Claude Code, 1 at a time on Codex (its runner serves one turn at a time, ADR 0020). The spend guard reserves each batch's estimate and keeps the answer call's estimate out of reach, so the sweep stops before the cap would block the answer.
4. **Pace and limits.** On a free model (`billing: free`, or a price of zero, as the house plan's chain) one request starts every 3.2 s (OpenRouter's free tier allows 20 a minute). A refused request (a rate limit, the whole chain busy, a CLI usage limit) waits 15, 30 and 60 s and then stops the sweep; OpenRouter's own fallback through the person's model chain happens inside each call. A turn makes at most `KB_CHAT_SWEEP_MAX_REQUESTS` model requests (default 250: planning, batches and the answer), and on the house plan no more than the house account's free requests left today (`HouseResearchRunner` passes them as `requestBudget`). No batch starts after the exhaustive profile's 30 minutes. "Parar" aborts everything and no call starts after it.
5. **Reduce and answer.** Only after the planned batches are done (or a ceiling stopped them) the orchestrator is asked, without tools and with the long output budget, for the answer from the merged findings (`sweepAnswerSystemPrompt`). Findings longer than one batch are first condensed by the model in parts (at most two rounds), then cut with a note. A stopped turn gets no answer.
6. **Coverage measured by the app.** `done` carries `coverage { target, phrases, documents, scanned, withHits|null, read, missing, batches, reason? }` computed from what really finished: the documents from the start of the set (in id order) up to the first one not fully swept. The prompts forbid coverage numbers, and `CoverageLineFilter` removes any line the model starts with "Cobertura" from the streamed answer. The web renders "Cobertura (medida pelo aplicativo): 2.555 documentos no conjunto · 2.555 varridos por dentro · 412 com ocorrências · 96 lidos." and, when something is left, "Varredura incompleta. Faltaram N documentos: <reason in words>." with "Continuar varredura".
7. **Cursor.** An unfinished sweep's `done` (and every progress event) carries `sweep { plan, after: last document id fully swept, totals, findings }`; `findings` (document id and text, at most 600 of 300 characters) are what the sweep found so far, so a continuation answers with them too. Progress events carry only the batch's new findings (`found`), which the page adds to its copy of the cursor, so a turn stopped on the page keeps everything found before the stop. A carried finding whose document is not in the set is dropped. The web saves it with the turn (`savedResearchTurnSchema.sweep`, with `coverage`), keeps the last progress cursor when the reader stops, and sends it back as `researchRequestSchema.sweep` (exhaustive only); the API validates it like any request body. It is the reader's own budget: a forged cursor only changes what that reader's own turn sweeps and shows (its totals are checked against the set's size, but a cursor that claims earlier turns read more is believed: accepted, not signed, since the page builds part of it). Documents added to the vault before the cursor's id after it was saved are not revisited (accepted; a new question covers them).
8. **Transparency.** Before the first batch a `sweep` event with `status: planned` gives documents, batches, estimated requests (planning + batches + 1) and whether the model is free; then one `running` event per finished batch. The web shows the estimate (with "Modelos gratuitos são menos rigorosos e mais lentos…" for free models) and "Lote 12 de 80 · 640 de 2.555 documentos" with a progress bar.
9. **Subscriptions.** Claude Code and Codex plan over the turn's MCP endpoint with `plan_sweep` added (`PlanningTools`); their text is never shown and the pass ends as soon as the plan is in. Each batch is its own CLI run with the batch prompt and no tools (Claude: `--tools ""`, the prompt in `--system-prompt`; Codex: the prompt first on stdin), and the answer is one more run whose text streams through the same filter. Codex keeps going through its sandbox runner with the arguments it builds itself; nothing in the runner, the container or the network changes (ADR 0020). Claude Code's exhaustive turns no longer get `--agents` or the Agent tool.

## Options considered

1. **App-driven map-reduce with a planned set (chosen).** The sweep's completeness no longer depends on the model; the cost is bounded and announced; the same code runs for every provider.
2. **Keep delegation, enforce coverage after the fact** (re-prompt until the model "covered" everything). Still the model's judgment; free models loop or stop; no bound on requests.
3. **Fixed per-question reports without a model** (ADR 0013 option 3). Exact for one shape of question only.
4. **Batches with tools (sub-researchers per batch).** More flexible, but each batch becomes a loop of unknown length: no estimate, and free models wander.

## Consequences

- Good: an exhaustive answer is built from every document of the plan, and the coverage the reader sees is measured, never claimed. A free model can no longer stop early.
- Good: one implementation (`runSweepTurn`, `SweepCoordinator`) for the in-API loop, Claude Code and Codex; Fast and Deep are unchanged.
- Bad (cost): a whole-set read is large (620 requests for the owner's question read whole on a free model; 161 with expressions; 32 on a subscription). The estimate says so before the first batch; the request cap, the house's free requests, the daily cap and the time cap stop the sweep with a cursor, and "Continuar varredura" spreads the rest over more turns (and days).
- Bad: one request per batch at 3.2 s on free models makes a 160-batch sweep take at least 8.5 minutes; it may need several continuations.
- Bad: batch quality depends on the model; findings are only as good as each batch's extraction, though every document is now seen.
- Removed: the `delegate` tool, the sub-agent loop and Claude Code's "pesquisador" agent (ADR 0014). The `delegate` step kind and `step.agent` stay in the contract so older event streams still validate.
- Contracts grow additively: `sweep` events, `done.coverage`, `done.sweep`, the request's `sweep`, the saved turn's `coverage` and `sweep`, the step kind `sweep`. Turns saved before stay valid. KBC-029's server-side turn record copies `coverage` and `sweep` from `done`, and a pending or interrupted turn keeps the last progress cursor.
- Config: `KB_CHAT_SWEEP_MAX_REQUESTS` (10–2,000, default 250); `RESEARCH_RATE_LIMIT` (research turns per minute per client, default 20, the old constant; the E2E server raises it).
- Compliance: unit tests with fake models (a lazy model that answers at once, a model that writes a false coverage line, caps, pacing, backoff, stop), CLI tests with the fake Claude Code and Codex (one run per batch, no `--agents`, Codex arguments locked down), E2E of the estimate, the progress, the coverage and the continuation at 1440 and 390 px, and CI's "Codex sandbox" proof job.
